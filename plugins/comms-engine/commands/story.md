# /story

The cycle writes a story at its end (`/social-cycle` step 7b); this writes
one now.

1. Spawn the `comms-engine:storyteller` agent.
2. Run `engine control start`: the control
   page opens on the story (its landing page), on this computer only.
3. Tell the person the cover line, the questions asked, and that they answer a
   question on its slide; the next story reads the answer.
