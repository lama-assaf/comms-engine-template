# brief: writing an X thread (salon:x-thread)

Use the skill for a thread's own craft: where a post ends, what the first post
must do alone, how the turn is placed. The piece itself is bound by this
engine's rules, which outrank the skill's habits:

- The beats are the format's `structure` in the stream's
  `.atelier/memory/formats.json`, in that order.
- Every post that states a checkable fact rests on a row of the stream's
  `claims.md`, listed in the item's `cites`. A post you cannot cite is cut or
  rewritten to design language.
- The thread says what we do not claim, in its own words, before the link.
- No hashtag and no emoji in the first post; the theme hashtag once, in the
  last. No mention of anyone not on a verified, cleared row of `handles.md`.
- A thread has no article: it is written into `teaser_x`, posts separated by a
  blank line, and `post` stays empty.

Nothing is posted. The thread is a draft that the gate reads like any other.
