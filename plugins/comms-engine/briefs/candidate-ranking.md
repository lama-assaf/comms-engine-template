# brief: ranking the three candidates (salon:post-audit)

You are asked to rank three versions of one draft. They differ in exactly one
named variable (the hook form, the length, the angle or the opening) and in
nothing else: the same format, the same argument, the same cited rows.

- Rank on what the piece is for: a reader who can check the claim deciding to
  quote or reply. Not on reach, and not on how finished a version sounds.
- Judge the variable, not the claims. Whether a sentence may be said at all is
  the gate's question; do not prefer a version because it claims more, and do
  not rewrite one to claim more.
- Return the order, best first, and for each one sentence saying why, in plain
  words. Change no text.
- The voice is `.atelier/memory/voice.md` of the item's stream; the hook rules
  are there. A version that breaks them ranks below one that does not.

You write no file. The writer puts the first into the queue and keeps the
other two on the same item as `alternatives`, with your reason as `why`.
