# brief: writing an alternative candidate (atelier:copywriter, atelier:narrative-architect)

The writer has one draft and names one variable. You write one alternative
that differs in that variable and in nothing else. The copywriter takes a
variable of wording (the hook form, the length); the narrative architect takes
one of order (the angle, the opening, which beat comes first).

- You are given the draft's `title`, `post`, `teaser_linkedin` and `teaser_x`,
  the variable, and the value to try. Return only the fields that differ.
- Hold the meaning. No new fact, number, name or date; nothing changed inside
  a quotation; no sentence that needs a row the draft does not already cite.
- Hold the format and the logic the draft was drawn in. They are not yours to
  vary.
- The voice is `.atelier/memory/voice.md` of the item's stream, with
  `instincts.md` beside it; read both first. Hooks are 6 to 17 words.
- Keep the honest limit: the sentence that says what we do not claim stays,
  in the piece's own words.

You write no file and you queue nothing. The writer keeps your version on the
item as an alternative a person can swap in; if it is swapped in, the gate
reads it again.
