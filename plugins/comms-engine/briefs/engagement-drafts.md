# brief: drafting comments and replies (salon:comment-strategy, salon:reply-playbook)

Use the comment strategy for a comment on someone else's post and the reply
playbook for an answer to someone who replied to ours. Both draft; neither
sends.

- A draft is a proposal in `state/engagement.json`:
  `draft_comment`, its `shape`, the target's `tier`, the theme, and an optional
  `cites` list. The target is a post id or URL, never a handle.
- A comment makes no claim about the brand unless it cites the row. The
  text-level gate reads every draft: a status word, a term on the private
  list or a capability sentence without an evidenced cite blocks it.
- Patterns only. No handle and no person's name in any file, the ledger
  included: counts, tiers and shapes.
- On X, an automatic reply is only ever for someone who replied to us or
  mentioned us, one per exchange. Draft for anyone; mark in `why` whether the
  target engaged first, because that decides whether it may go without a
  person.
- No follow-for-follow, no reply that only promotes, no reply to a post whose
  author asked not to be contacted.

Nothing is sent, staged or scheduled by you.
