# brief: the hypothesis of a draft (atelier:ab-test-design)

Use the skill to state one testable difference, and only one, for a draft:
the variable its three candidates differ in.

- One variable per hypothesis: the hook form, the format, the length, or the
  angle. Never two; a test that changes two things teaches nothing.
- Write it as a sentence a result can contradict: "a two-sentence contrast
  hook beats a single declarative on this theme".
- The measure is this engine's own: the item's engagement index against the
  stream's baseline for its platform and format, scored at seven days, as the
  band `below`, `baseline` or `above`. Do not propose another metric, a sample
  size the calendar cannot give, or a paid test.
- Organic channels test in matched sequential slots over weeks. No audience is
  split and nothing is sent twice.

You write no file. The writer puts the sentence in `hypothesis`, the band it
expects in `predicted` and the live test's id, or `none`, in `test_id`.
